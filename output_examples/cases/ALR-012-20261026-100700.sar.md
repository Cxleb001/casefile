## Narrative

Lyons & Rollins Partners (223:811A00890), a Saudi real‑estate partnership with a declared turnover of $18 M, moved $18.2 M outbound in ten days while receiving only $0.8 M inbound – a 22.6× pass‑through ratio. Network analysis uncovered a 4‑hop cycle that returns funds to the account (23,370 SAR out, 51,610 SAR back) within a week, together with two one‑off ACH inbound transfers and a subsequent layering step. Outflows project to $56 M/month, three times the declared turnover. No convergence, inter‑counterparty links, or uniform‑amount patterns were observed, and the sole high‑volume hub carries no value. Watchlist screening shows no match for the subject; counterparty name‑only hits are mismatched on DOB/country. Twelve Institution B counterparties remain unscreened. The combination of a returning money‑cycle, fan‑in, layering and extreme pass‑through constitutes a strong structural money‑laundering indicator, warranting escalation.
