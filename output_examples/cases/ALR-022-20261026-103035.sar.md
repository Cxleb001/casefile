## Narrative

Ortiz & Cole LLP (214615:8075AC7C0), a low‑risk US restaurant partnership, received $111.6 k from 15 counterparties and sent $112.9 M to 16 counterparties between 1‑10 Sep 2022. The outbound flow is dominated by a single $104.9 M ACH to an Australian real‑estate partnership. Network analysis shows a gather‑scatter structure: within three days the account made 10 one‑off outbound transfers and received 10 one‑off inbound transfers with no other activity to those parties, and six of those one‑off recipients forwarded the funds onward within 72 hours, constituting a layering chain. The out‑to‑in ratio exceeds 1,000 and a burst of 12 counterparties appeared in the peak 24‑hour period. No cycles, convergence, or inter‑counterparty payments were found, and declared turnover is broadly consistent. However, the combination of one‑off transfers, rapid layering, extreme pass‑through, and burst activity meets the escalation threshold for a gather‑scatter typology. Thirty‑three B‑side counterparties remain unscreened due to B’s governed view. Accordingly, the case is escalated for further investigation.
